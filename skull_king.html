Scoring rules
  • Round N deals N cards. Each player bids how many tricks they'll win.
  • Bid > 0, hit exactly: +20 per trick won.
  • Bid 0, hit exactly (won none): +10 × round number.
  • Missed (over or under): −10 per trick off. A missed zero-bid loses 10 × round number.
  • Bonus (14s captured, Skull King / Pirates / Mermaid captures) is added on top — enter the total bonus per player.
